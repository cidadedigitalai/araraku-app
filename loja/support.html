How do I play?

  • Each color on the board is a tree and gets exactly one macaw.
  • There can be only one macaw per row and one per column.
  • Macaws never perch next to each other, not even diagonally.
  • The macaw with a white border is already in place: start from it.

An animated tutorial plays the first time you open the game. To watch it again, open the settings (the sliders button at the top) and tap "Watch the tutorial".

How do I land a macaw?

Tap a square once to mark it with an X (a spot where the macaw cannot be). Double-tap to land the macaw. To remove a macaw, tap it again.

What happens when I make a mistake?

Each mistake costs one nut. You have three per level; when they run out, the level starts over. Every macaw has a logical reason to be where it is, so it pays to think before guessing.

I'm stuck. What now?

Use the light bulb button for a hint and the arrow button to undo your last move. In the settings, the "Auto-mark X" option automatically marks the squares around each macaw you land.

How do I change the language?

Use the EN and PT buttons at the top of the game. The game remembers your choice. On first launch it follows your iPhone's language.

How do I turn off the sound or the music?

Open the settings (the sliders button at the top) and turn off "Sound effects" (the macaws' song and the mistake sounds) or "Background music". The game remembers your choice.

How do I erase my progress and start over?

Your progress is saved only on your device. To start from scratch, delete the app and install it again.

I got a new iPhone. Does my progress come with me?

No. Since the game has no account and sends nothing over the internet, your progress stays on the device where you played.

Does it need internet? Are there purchases or ads?

It doesn't need internet, has no purchases and no ads.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 15 or later. On iPad, the game opens in iPhone mode.

What about my privacy?

Ararakú collects no data at all. See the privacy policy.
